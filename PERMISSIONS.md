# Permissions for LarvIDE

LarvIDE is **Source-Available** under the `LarvIDE Educational Source-Available Licence v1.0` (`LICENSE`).

## TL;DR

| Action | Need permission? |
| :--- | :--- |
| Use / Install / Run for study or assignments (student, university, anyone) | **No** — always allowed |
| Modify privately for yourself (fix a bug, learn, keep it on your device) | **No** |
| Share an *unmodified* copy (keep copyright & licence intact, don't claim it's yours) | **No** |
| **Publish / Distribute a *modified* version** (GitHub fork, Play Store build, APK for your class/company) | **Yes — written permission required** |

This applies to **everyone**, including universities, schools, and companies.

> **Universities / Schools — Required Attribution:** Even when you use the *unmodified* official build in a classroom or lab, please give visible credit to students as required by `LICENSE` §4(e): **`Project Developed By Abd Allah Al Habbash — github.com/HabbashX`** (ideally clickable) in your course material, lab handout, or by keeping LarvIDE's About screen intact, so students know who built the tool for them.

## How to request permission

Send an email to **abdalahalhabbash@gmail.com** with:

1.  Who you are (name / university / company)
2.  What you want to modify
3.  How/where you will distribute it (GitHub repo URL, Play Store listing, internal university portal, etc.)
4.  Whether you will use the `LarvIDE` name/logo or a distinct name

The author will reply within a few days. Permission may include a condition such as using a distinct name like `LarvIDE — University of X Edition` or `Based on LarvIDE by HabbashX`.

## Why this model?

LarvIDE was built so students in Gaza without laptops can pass their assignments. The source is open for learning. The permission gate exists only to prevent someone from cloning the project, changing a colour, and re-publishing it as their own work — while keeping your ability to study and fix it privately fully intact.

## Students: Pull Requests

You are welcome to send Pull Requests to `HabbashX/LarvIDE` to *improve the official project*. By submitting a contribution you agree it will be licensed under the same LarvIDE Licence and may be included without additional permission. You do **not** need separate permission to open a PR against the official repository.

## Branding

Even with permission to distribute a modified version, you must follow `TRADEMARK.md`: do not present your fork as the official `LarvIDE` without explicit branding permission. Use a distinct name/logo for independent distributions.
