## 7. Use of the Larv IDE Name

You may use the name **Larv IDE** when reasonably referring to the original project or explaining that your software is based on Larv IDE.

You may not use "Larv IDE" as the primary product name of a modified or independently distributed version in a way that suggests that the modified version is the official Larv IDE.

For example, the following are generally acceptable descriptive uses:

> "Based on Larv IDE."

> "A fork of Larv IDE."

> "Compatible with Larv IDE."

The following uses are not permitted without authorization:

> "Larv IDE Pro" as the name of an independently distributed modified product.

> "Official Larv IDE" when the product is not officially released by HabbashX.

> A product using the Larv IDE logo in a way that suggests official endorsement.
